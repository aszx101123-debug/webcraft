Your world, your way

A Minecraft-inspired sandbox built with JavaScript, Three.js and WebGL.

Survive your first night

Mine resources, craft tools, grow crops, cook food and face hostile mobs. Keep an eye on your hearts, hunger and underwater air bubbles.

Build without limits

Use creative mode's unlimited blocks and flight to build a home, a tower or a whole village. Switch modes from the game menu.

Explore 16 biomes

Find forests, deserts, snowy terrain and more in seeded worlds with caves, ores, weather and a day/night cycle. Share a seed to explore the same terrain in your own single-player worlds.

Worlds save in your current browser. Export a backup from the Esc menu to move a world to another browser or device.

Survive and build together

Create a room, choose survival or creative and share the invite link. Up to 4 players share blocks, mobs, drops, chests and furnaces. No login is required.

Keep the host’s game tab open. Menus do not pause the shared world. Worlds save in the host’s browser; your inventory and survival stats stay on your own device. Invite people you trust; this is cooperative play without PvP.

On a phone or tablet?

Use the on-screen joystick to move, drag to look around, tap to use or place, and hold to mine. Hold the jump button to swim up.

Before you jump in

Do I need an account?

Both game modes work without signing in. Daily credit rewards and badge exchanges are for signed-in accounts; guests do not receive credits.

Where is my world saved?

In your current browser, rather than an account cloud. Use the backup controls in the Esc menu before clearing browser data or changing devices.

Is this official Minecraft?

No. WebCraft is an independent, Minecraft-inspired fan project and is not affiliated with Mojang Studios or Microsoft.
